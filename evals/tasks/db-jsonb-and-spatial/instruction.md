# Slow document and location queries

Two queries show sequential scans in production (PostgreSQL + PostGIS):

- `SELECT ... FROM documents WHERE metadata @> '{"source": "upload"}'`
- `SELECT ... FROM locations WHERE ST_DWithin(geom, :point, :radius)`

Both tables are large and read-heavy. Add suitable indexes to the SQLAlchemy models in `/app/app/models/` (migrations are autogenerated from the models later; no migration needed).
